## What is an Online Barcode Scanner?

An online barcode scanner is a web-based tool that uses your device’s camera (phone or laptop) to read barcodes and QR codes instantly inside the browser.  
All processing happens on your device (client-side), so no data is sent to any server.

## How to Use ScanBarcode Pro

1. Open [https://scanbarcode-pro.vercel.app/scanner.html](https://scanbarcode-pro.vercel.app/scanner.html)
2. Click **Mulai Kamera** (Start Camera)
3. Allow camera permission
4. Point the camera at the barcode
5. Result appears automatically

What Is an Online Barcode Scanner?

An online barcode scanner is a web-based tool that instantly reads and decodes information from barcodes and QR codes using your device's camera — no app download, no registration, and no complicated setup required. Simply open your browser, allow camera access, and point at any barcode or QR code. The result appears automatically with complete information.

Supported Barcode Formats

ScanBarcode Pro supports over 50 different barcode and QR code formats. Here are the most commonly used ones:

  • 1D Barcodes (Linear): EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-A, UPC-E, Code128, Code39, Code93, ITF-14, Codabar, MSI Plessey
  • 2D Barcodes (Matrix): QR Code, DataMatrix, PDF417, Aztec Code, MaxiCode, DotCode
  • Special Formats: WiFi QR Code, vCard, ISBN, ISSN, GS1 DataBar

Each format serves a different purpose. EAN-13 is the international standard for retail products, Code128 powers logistics and shipping labels, QR Codes are ideal for digital content and links, and DataMatrix is widely used in industrial applications. ScanBarcode Pro detects the format automatically — no manual configuration needed.
